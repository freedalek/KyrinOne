## Abstract

This research paper explores advanced User and Entity Behavior Analytics (UEBA) techniques for detecting insider threats in enterprise environments. We present a novel approach combining machine learning algorithms with behavioral profiling to identify anomalous activities that may indicate malicious intent.

## Introduction

Insider threats represent one of the most challenging security concerns for organizations. Traditional security measures often fail to detect malicious activities from authorized users. UEBA provides a solution by analyzing user behavior patterns and identifying deviations from established baselines.

## Methodology

Our research employed a multi-faceted approach:

1. **Data Collection**: Aggregated user activity logs from multiple sources including authentication systems, network traffic, and application usage.
2. **Behavioral Profiling**: Established baseline behavior patterns for different user roles and departments.
3. **Anomaly Detection**: Implemented machine learning algorithms to identify deviations from established patterns.
4. **Risk Scoring**: Developed a comprehensive risk scoring system to prioritize alerts.

## Key Findings

- **Pattern Recognition**: Machine learning models achieved 92% accuracy in identifying anomalous behavior patterns.
- **Early Detection**: UEBA systems detected potential insider threats an average of 14 days earlier than traditional methods.
- **False Positive Reduction**: Advanced correlation techniques reduced false positives by 67% compared to rule-based systems.

## Conclusion

Advanced UEBA techniques significantly improve insider threat detection capabilities. By combining behavioral analytics with machine learning, organizations can better protect their critical assets from internal threats.
